About

RAR is proprietary (WinRAR's format), which is why operating systems do not open it natively — but the libarchive library reads both RAR4 and RAR5, and this page runs it as WebAssembly entirely in your browser. The RAR is unpacked in memory and repacked as a standard ustar TAR with the same paths and bytes. RAR-specific features (recovery records, solid compression) are archive-level and do not transfer; encrypted RARs are rejected with a clear message. The sibling RAR to ZIP converter on this site targets Windows-friendly output instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are RAR5 files supported?

Yes — libarchive reads RAR4 and RAR5, including multi-volume sets if you provide the first volume with all parts present in the same archive file.

Why convert RAR to TAR at all?

Servers, Docker contexts and Unix backup pipelines speak TAR natively; RAR needs extra tooling everywhere it goes.
