Password Strength Checker - Entropy, Feedback and Crack Time

In short: the Password Strength Checker estimates your password entropy in bits from its length and character pool, applies open penalties for repeats and sequences, and shows specific fixes plus a crack-time estimate. Your password is analysed locally and is never sent, saved or logged anywhere.

Most strength meters are a black box that says "weak" and leaves you guessing. This one shows its working: the exact pool maths, the penalties, and what to change - computed entirely on your own device, which is the only place a password should ever be typed into a tool.

How to use the Password Strength Checker

  1. Type or paste the password into the box - analysis happens live, entirely on your device.
  2. Read the entropy in bits and the strength label.
  3. Work through the feedback list: each item is a concrete fix that raises the estimate.
  4. Clear the box when you are done. Nothing was ever transmitted or stored.

How it works

The checker uses the standard information-theory estimate, in the open: it detects which character pools your password draws from (lowercase 26, uppercase 26, digits 10, symbols 33), and computes entropy = length x log2(pool). It then applies two transparent penalties: a run of 3 or more repeated characters, and an ascending or descending sequence of 4 or more characters, each reduce the effective entropy by 20%, because guessing attacks try those shapes early. The score bands are <28 bits very weak, <36 weak, <60 fair, <90 strong, and 90+ excellent. Crack time assumes 10 billion guesses per second - a fast offline attack - and halves the keyspace, as an attacker finds the password halfway through on average.

Worked example

"password" is 8 lowercase letters: pool 26, raw entropy 37.6 bits - already only fair, before you consider that it sits at the top of every leaked-password list, which no formula can see. Four random words with a digit and a symbol pass 90 bits easily, and are easier to remember: length is the strongest lever you have.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this password strength checker free?

Yes - completely free, with no sign-up and no daily cap.

Is it safe to type a real password here?

The analysis runs 100% in your browser: the password is never sent to a server, never stored, and never written to any log - you can verify that by loading the page and going offline before typing. Even so, best practice is to test a password shaped like yours rather than your exact banking password.

How accurate is the entropy estimate?

It is an honest model, not an oracle. Pool-based entropy assumes random characters; human-chosen passwords carry patterns a formula cannot fully see - which is why this checker also flags repeats and sequences, and why a password manager generating truly random passwords is always the strongest option.

Why is there no dictionary or zxcvbn check?

This tool deliberately uses a small, transparent scoring function whose maths you can read on this page, instead of a large opaque library. Dictionary words are the gap: "correct horse battery staple" scores high on length yet uses real words - treat the estimate as a ceiling for human-chosen passwords.

What makes the biggest difference?

Length. Every extra character multiplies the guessing work by the pool size. A long passphrase you can remember beats a short tangle of symbols you cannot.
