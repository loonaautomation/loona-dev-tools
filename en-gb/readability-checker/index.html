SEO Readability Checker - Six Scores for Web Copy

In short: the SEO Readability Checker scores pasted web copy with six established formulas - Flesch Ease, Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog, Coleman-Liau, SMOG and ARI - plus the sentence statistics that actually drive them, framed for search content rather than school homework.

Scope note: Text Tools has a Reading Level Calculator covering Flesch Ease and Grade Level for general writing. This checker is the SEO-content version - six formulas, sentence-length diagnostics and web-audience targets - which is why it lives with the webmaster tools.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your input is never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the SEO Readability Checker

  1. Paste your page copy or article draft (a few paragraphs score most reliably).
  2. Click Score readability.
  3. Compare the six scores - they should broadly agree; a wild outlier points at very long words or sentences.
  4. Fix the longest sentences first; that moves every score at once.

How it works

Each formula weighs the same raw ingredients differently: Flesch scores use sentence length and syllables per word; Gunning Fog counts words of three or more syllables ('complex words'); Coleman-Liau uses letters instead of syllables, so it is the hardest to game; SMOG was designed for healthcare text; ARI uses characters per word. Syllables are counted with the standard vowel-group heuristic. Short samples swing wildly, so score full sections, not single sentences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this checker free?

Yes - completely free, no sign-up.

How is this different from the Text Tools Reading Level Calculator?

That tool covers two scores for general writing. This one is built for SEO content work: six formulas plus sentence-length diagnostics and web-audience targets, in the webmaster suite.

What score should web copy aim for?

Flesch Ease around 60-70 (plain English) suits most audiences. Technical and legal content legitimately scores lower.

Do readability scores affect ranking directly?

Google says readability is not a direct ranking factor - but readable copy keeps visitors reading, which is the point of publishing it.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Scoring runs 100% in your browser.
